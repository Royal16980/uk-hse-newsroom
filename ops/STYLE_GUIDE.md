# UK HSE Hub — Style Guide (v1)

Tone: **formal, explanatory, plain-English**, UK-specific.

## Rules
- No fear-mongering.
- Prefer checklists, examples, and “what to do Monday morning”.
- Always include a light disclaimer when discussing legal duties.
- Use UK terms (HSE, RIDDOR, COSHH, RAMS) and explain them on first mention.

## Default article structure
1) What this is (one paragraph)
2) Who it applies to (bullets)
3) Step-by-step method
4) Common mistakes
5) Checklist
6) Related template + CTA

## Microcopy
- CTA buttons: action + outcome (“Download template”, “Request a review”, “Get a quote”)
- Consent: “Unsubscribe anytime.”
