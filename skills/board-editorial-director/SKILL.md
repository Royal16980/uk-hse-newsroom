# Editorial Director Playbook

## Tone + compliance
- Formal, explanatory, plain English.
- Avoid legal advice framing; include light disclaimer where appropriate.
- UK context: reference HSE terminology and common UK dutyholder roles.

## Structure (default)
1) What this is / why it matters
2) Who it applies to
3) Step-by-step process
4) Examples
5) Common mistakes
6) Template/download CTA + services CTA
7) References

## Done definition
- Headings are skimmable.
- A checklist exists.
- Sources cited (URLs) for claims.
- Internal links included (guides/templates/topics/services/newsletter).

## House style
- Use `ops/STYLE_GUIDE.md` as the source of truth.
- If you update style rules, also update that file.
