# Research Director Runbook (UK H&S)

## Outputs (choose one)
- **Content Brief** (for Writer): outline, audience, intent, key points, UK citations, internal links.
- **Cluster Map**: pillar + supporting articles + suggested URL slugs.
- **Source Pack**: 5–10 authoritative links with 1–2 line notes.

## Rules
- Prefer primary sources: HSE, legislation.gov.uk, ACOPs, GOV.UK.
- Capture sources in `ops/SOURCES.md` (add new ones).
- Keep it practical: what the reader must do, not history.
