# Leadership Post Template (LinkedIn-style; also usable as IG caption)

## Hooks (3)
1) "If you can't answer this, you're managing risk blind."
2) "The cost isn't the incident. It's the uncertainty before it."
3) "Leaders don't need more safety posters. They need better signals."

## Short version (60-120 words)
- 1 line: what leaders must know
- 2-3 bullets: if you don't know
- 1 line CTA: ask for evidence / decide control

## Medium version (150-250 words)
- Short story or scenario
- Translate hazard -> exposure -> business impact
- 3 bullets: what to do this week
- CTA

## Hashtags (5-10)
Keep minimal and relevant.
