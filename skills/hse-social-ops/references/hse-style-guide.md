# HSE Style Guide (Health & Safety)

## Tone
- Clear, practical, no fluff.
- Confident but not arrogant.
- UK-first wording unless told otherwise.

## Do
- Use concrete examples: "wet floor near loading bay".
- Use hierarchy of controls (eliminate -> substitute -> engineer -> admin -> PPE).
- Encourage reporting near-misses.
- Keep steps short; prefer checklists.

## Don't
- No medical diagnosis/treatment advice.
- No legal guarantees (say "check your local guidance" / "consult a competent person").
- Avoid fearmongering.

## Calls to action (safe)
- "Save this for your next toolbox talk"
- "Comment 'CHECKLIST' and I'll send the template"
- "Follow for weekly H&S tips"

## Common compliance anchors (UK)
- Risk assessment (Management Regs)
- COSHH
- PUWER
- LOLER
- Work at Height
- Fire safety basics

If the request involves a regulated claim, add a short disclaimer line.
